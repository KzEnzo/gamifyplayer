GTA 6 is coming soon, and there are a few things you can do right now to get ready.

## What to Do Now
1. **Link your Rockstar Social Club account** — make sure it’s active and verified.
2. **Back up your GTA Online progress** — some items may carry over to the new game.
3. **Check your storage space** — the game is expected to be over 100GB, so free up space now.
4. **Update your graphics drivers** — this will save you time on launch day.

## What You Can’t Do Yet
- Pre-order bonuses haven’t been announced.
- No official information about character transfers.

We’ll update this brief as more details are revealed.
