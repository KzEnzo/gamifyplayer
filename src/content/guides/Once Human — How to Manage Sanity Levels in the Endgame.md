## Problem
Your sanity keeps dropping fast, and you don’t know how to stop the negative effects from killing you.

## What we verified
Tested multiple sanity management methods in endgame zones.

## Steps
1. **Carry sanity-restoring items** — always keep at least 5 in your inventory before entering high-corruption areas.
2. **Stay near light sources** — campfires and torches slow sanity drain significantly.
3. **Avoid looking at the distorted sky or shadow monsters** — they cause a big sanity drop.
4. **Sleep in a bed every night** — it resets your sanity to full and removes temporary debuffs.
5. **Use sanity protection gear** — some armor pieces reduce corruption damage by 30-50%.

## Failure modes
If your sanity hits zero, you will start seeing hallucinations that attack you, and eventually die if you don’t restore it quickly.
