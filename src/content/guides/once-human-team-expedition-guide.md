## Problem
Random teams have no role division, get wiped easily and waste time in dungeons.

## What we verified
Tested 4-player party compositions and task division for smoother runs.

## Steps
1. Split roles: one tank, one healer/sanity support, two damage dealers.
2. Let tank pull monsters first; others attack from safe distance.
3. Assign one player to collect loot quickly to avoid missing rare drops.
4. Keep one person watching surroundings to prevent surprise enemy ambushes.
5. Share sanity food and healing items equally to keep the whole team stable.

## Failure modes
All players charging in randomly without roles will cause chaotic fights and frequent team wipes.
