## Problem

The eastern ridge **looks climbable** but often dumps players into an out-of-bounds reset.

## Verified route

1. Start from the **Signal bonfire** — not the cliff marker.
2. Hug the **white lichen band** until the camera widens (that is the safe seam).
3. Drop onto the wooden platform **before** the hawk cue — co-op tether breaks if you jump after the cue.

## Co-op

Player two should anchor **3m behind** the lead — tether snap is inconsistent if you stack vertically.
