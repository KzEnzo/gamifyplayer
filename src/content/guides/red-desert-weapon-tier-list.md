## Problem
You don’t know which weapons to pick up early on, and want to avoid wasting time on bad guns.

## What we verified
Tested all available weapons in PvE and low-tier PvP scenarios.

## Steps / Tier List
1. **S-Tier (Best All-Round)**
   - Assault Rifle: Great damage, accuracy, and ammo availability.
   - Shotgun: One-shot kill potential at close range, perfect for base defense.

2. **A-Tier (Situational Picks)**
   - Hunting Rifle: Good for long-range PvE but too slow for PvP.
   - SMG: High fire rate but burns through ammo quickly.

3. **B-Tier (Avoid Unless No Other Option)**
   - Pistol: Only use as a last resort backup weapon.

## Failure modes
Using a low-tier weapon in open-world PvP will almost always result in you losing the fight. Stick to S or A tier.
