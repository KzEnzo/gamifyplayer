## Problem
Default settings cause slow reaction, lag vision and awkward key layout in PvP.

## What we verified
Tested dozens of sensitivity and keybind layouts for close-quarters and long-range combat.

## Steps
1. Lower unnecessary graphic effects to boost frame rate and reduce blur during fights.
2. Adjust mouse sensitivity to a medium value for steady aiming and fast turning.
3. Bind quick heal, weapon swap and crouch to easy-to-reach side keys.
4. Disable unnecessary vibration and screen shake for clearer vision.
5. Keep crosshair simple and minimal to avoid blocking enemy targets.

## Failure modes
Keeping default high graphics and messy keybinds will make you react slower and lose most 1v1 encounters.
