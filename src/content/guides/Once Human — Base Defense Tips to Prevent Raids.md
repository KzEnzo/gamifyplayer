## Problem
Your base is getting raided too often, and you want to make it harder for other players to steal your loot.

## What we verified
Tested base designs against common raiding strategies.

## Steps
1. **Build your base on high ground** — raiders have a harder time setting up ladders or ramps.
2. **Use reinforced walls on the first floor** — they take much longer to break.
3. **Place storage containers in the center of your base** — far from walls, so raiders can’t reach them quickly.
4. **Add traps near entrances** — bear traps and electric fences slow raiders down.
5. **Hide your most valuable loot** — use hidden compartments under floors or behind walls.

## Failure modes
If you leave storage containers near the outer walls, raiders can break in and steal everything in seconds.
