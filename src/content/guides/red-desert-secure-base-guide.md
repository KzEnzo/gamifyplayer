## Problem
Your base keeps getting raided, and you want to build something that keeps your loot safe.

## What we verified
Tested different base designs against common raiding methods.

## Steps
1. **Choose a hidden location** — avoid flat, open areas. Build near rocks or cliffs for natural cover.
2. **Layer your defenses** — start with a low outer wall, then a second higher wall around your main storage.
3. **Place traps at the entrance** — tripwires and landmines will slow down raiders.
4. **Store your most valuable loot in the center** — far from the walls, where raiders can’t easily reach.
5. **Use metal doors and walls** — they take much longer to break than wood.

## Failure modes
If you build a simple square base with only one layer of walls, it will get raided quickly. Always add multiple layers.
