## Problem
Players randomly assign talent points and end up weak in both combat and survival.

## What we verified
Tested multiple talent builds for solo play, team dungeon and PvP scenarios.

## Steps
1. Prioritize **survival and sanity talents** first for early game stability.
2. Add combat damage and weapon efficiency points once your survival is stable.
3. Reserve a few points for gathering and crafting speed to save farming time.
4. Do not spread points too thin; focus on one main playstyle first.
5. Keep one free respec scroll ready in case you want to switch build later.

## Failure modes
Spreading talent points evenly across every branch results in no clear strength and weak overall performance.
