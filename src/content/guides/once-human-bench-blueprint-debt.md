## Problem

Players chase bench tiers and **stall blueprint completion** right before seasonal resets.

## Rule of thumb

If you are **more than 2 tiers** below the seasonal cap **and** you still have 8+ uncrafted blues, stop upgrading the bench until you clear debt.

## Table

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Vendor sells rare solvents | Buy first, bench second |
| Daily reset in <2h | Finish crafts, do not start upgrades |
| Co-op partner offline | Avoid chained crafts (desync risk) |
