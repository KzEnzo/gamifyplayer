## Problem
New players waste hours wandering randomly and level up very slowly.

## What we verified
Tested multiple spawn routes and XP gain rates to find the most efficient path.

## Steps
1. Start by completing all **starter tutorial missions** first, they give massive base XP.
2. Focus on killing **low-level neutral NPCs** along the main road, safe and easy XP.
3. Gather basic resources while traveling — gathering also gives steady XP.
4. Avoid high-PvP zones at low level; dying loses XP and wastes time.
5. Turn in daily small quests every day to stack bonus XP.

## Failure modes
Running straight into high-level monster areas or PvP hotspots will get you killed repeatedly and slow down leveling progress heavily.
