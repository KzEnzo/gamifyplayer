## Problem
You’ve entered the open world for the first time, and you don’t know how to avoid dying or losing all your loot to enemies.

## What we verified
Tested in the early game starting zone. New players are often caught off-guard by fast melee enemies and ambushes from other players.

## Steps
1. When you spawn, **stay near the road or safe zone** until you have at least one weapon and basic armor.
2. Always check your **mini-map for red dots** — those are enemy players or hostile NPCs.
3. If you see an enemy approaching, **run to the nearest rock or building** to block their line of sight.
4. Use your **quick-heal item immediately** when you take damage — don’t wait until you’re low.
5. If you’re about to die, **run away instead of fighting** — you can come back later to recover your gear.

## Failure modes
If you stand still in the open field, you will get targeted and killed quickly. Keep moving and use cover.
