## Problem
Players ignore common-looking items, missing out on strong utility and trading value.

## What we verified
Checked item crafting chains, trader prices and practical combat usage of overlooked drops.

## Steps
1. Keep all basic repair parts — always in demand for weapon and base fixing.
2. Stock up on simple medical items; they are cheap to carry and save your life often.
3. Save uncommon junk items — many are required for high-tier crafting recipes.
4. Do not sell low-tier ammo casually; late game ammo is harder to farm.
5. Store extra clothing items — many can be traded for rare materials later.

## Failure modes
Blindly selling all common drops early will force you to farm basic materials again later, wasting hours.
