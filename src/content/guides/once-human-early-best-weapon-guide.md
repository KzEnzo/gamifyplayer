## Problem
New players stick to weak basic weapons and struggle with monsters and events.

## What we verified
Tested weapon drop locations, quest rewards and earliest accessible strong gear.

## Steps
1. Finish main story early chapters to get a free reliable baseline weapon.
2. Clear small hidden instance rooms marked on the map for rare weapon drops.
3. Save seasonal currency to exchange for a guaranteed good early weapon.
4. Avoid wasting resources upgrading temporary low-tier guns.
5. Team up for one elite world boss kill for a high chance of rare weapon drop.

## Failure modes
Wasting lots of upgrade materials on temporary basic weapons will slow your early game progress significantly.
