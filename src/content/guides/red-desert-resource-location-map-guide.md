## Problem
Players cannot find rare resources and keep running in circles looking for ore and materials.

## What we verified
Marked all fixed spawn nodes and confirmed safe low-conflict farming zones.

## Steps
1. Metal ore mostly spawns around **rock cliff areas** and mountain edges.
2. Wood and plant resources concentrate in small oasis green zones.
3. Rare high-value materials only refresh in **remote hidden valleys**.
4. Plan a loop route instead of random searching to save travel time.
5. Mark every node you find in-game for future fast farming.

## Failure modes
Searching open flat desert areas randomly almost never spawns rare resources, wasting lots of playtime.
