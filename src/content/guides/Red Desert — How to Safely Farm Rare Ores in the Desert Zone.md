## Problem
You need to farm rare ores in the open desert, but don’t want to lose your loot to roaming gank squads or enemy players.

## What we verified
Checked on **Steam PC** with default settings and PvP-enabled servers. Enemy players can use stealth to ambush ore nodes, so timing and route are critical.

## Steps
1.  Only farm nodes during **off-peak hours** (early morning or late night) to avoid high-traffic gank routes.
2.  Use the **rock outcrop** nodes — they block line of sight and give you a 2-second reaction window if someone approaches.
3.  Before mining, **check the local chat and kill feed** for nearby enemy activity; if you see red player names, move to another node immediately.
4.  Keep a **mount or fast travel scroll** ready in your inventory to escape if you get jumped.

## Failure modes
If you see a player camping the node from a distance, **do not start mining** — they’re waiting for you to commit before attacking. Move to a different zone.
